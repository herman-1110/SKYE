01 · Problem Statement

Manual Safety Monitoring Doesn't Scale

  • No real-time location awareness — supervisors cannot tell where guards and workers are inside large industrial floors without calling them.
  • Delayed incident response — a worker who collapses or is injured may lie undetected for minutes until the next manual check.
  • Patrol compliance is unverifiable — there is no automated way to confirm guards visited required checkpoints or lingered long enough.
  • Ghost patrol risk — BLE badges can be left at a station; CCTV alone cannot distinguish a present person from an unattended badge.
  • Post-shift analysis is manual — safety officers write reports from memory and logbooks, missing patterns and under-reporting near-misses.

04 · Positioning

LDPL + Kalman Filter Pipeline

Step 1 — RSSI → Distance

d = 10 ^ ((TxPower − RSSI) / (10 × n))

Log-distance path loss model. Path loss exponent n tunable via env var.

Step 2 — Multilateration

Least-squares across all N APs simultaneously via numpy.linalg.lstsq. Result clamped to floor bounds before Kalman input.

Step 3 — Kalman Smoothing

State vector: [x, y, vx, vy]

  • 8m outlier rejection before update
  • Clamped to floor bounds after each tick
  • Reset if gap > 10 s (stale signal)
Step 4 — Collision Prediction

predict_ahead(3) projects positions forward 3 s using velocity. Direction-aware — diverging entities do not trigger.

05 · Safety Alerts

Four Alert Types

Man Down

Worker or guard position timestamp becomes stale (> MAN_DOWN_MINUTES). Fires regardless of zone. Suppressed 30 s after first alert. Forklifts exempt.

Collision

Worker's Kalman-predicted position converges with forklift's predicted position within threshold. Direction-aware — both parties stored (other_person_id). 30 s suppression per pair.

Ghost Patrol

BLE tag is detected at checkpoint but VIGI camera does NOT confirm human presence. Fires only when BLE present AND VIGI absent — not the reverse.

Patrol Violation

Guard misses a checkpoint (actual_arrival is null) or dwell time falls below MIN_DWELL_SECONDS. Triggers immediately on patrol log write.

06 · AI Reports

Gemini-Powered Audit Reports

Patrol Report
  • Per-guard, per-shift
  • Inputs: patrol log checkpoints + RTDB alerts filtered by guard ID
  • Output: safety rating 1–10, risk findings, corrective actions
Safety Event Report
  • Per-person, per-date
  • Inputs: all RTDB alerts for that person on that day
  • Output: incident summary, risk assessment, behavioural patterns, corrective actions, safety rating
LLM Pipeline
  • Model: Gemini 2.5 Flash (default)
  • Pluggable: Gemini · OpenAI · Claude · Ollama via LLM_PROVIDER env var
  • Formatting constrained — no ####, no backticks, no HTML from LLM
  • RAG service stubbed — text-embedding-004 upgrade pending
Rendering
  • Custom SimpleMarkdown renderer in Next.js
  • Handles #–##### headings, **bold**, *italic*, `code`, bullet/ordered lists
  • Copy-to-clipboard on report panel
